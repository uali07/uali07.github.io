I am a mechanical engineer and interdisciplinary researcher with more than eleven years of experience in engineering research and university teaching. My work integrates high-fidelity computational fluid dynamics, fluid–structure interaction, machine learning, high-performance computing, and experimental validation to develop intelligent flow-control technologies and sustainable energy and water systems.

I hold a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from Khalifa University, where my thesis examined heat transfer and flow-induced vibrations of circular cylinders in crossflow. Since then my research has extended into offshore platform optimization, shape memory alloy vortex generators for dry cooling, sloshing control in partially filled tanks, and blended-wing-body UAV design.

To date I have authored 25 published journal articles and 19 conference papers, and I am an inventor on one granted U.S. patent and two filed patent applications.

## Research areas

**Fluid–structure interaction, vibration, and flow control.** Flow- and vortex-induced vibrations of bluff bodies and offshore and aerospace structures. Passive and active flow control for vibration suppression, heat transfer enhancement, and energy harvesting. Sloshing dynamics and control using nature-inspired baffle designs.

**AI, data-driven modelling, and HPC simulation.** Hybrid machine-learning models for oil and gas production forecasting and offshore-system optimization. Data-driven modelling with SINDy to identify governing behaviour in wake flow, supported by parallel simulation on HPC systems.

**Smart materials, thermofluids, and sustainable thermal systems.** Shape memory alloy vortex generators for heat transfer enhancement, thermoacoustic engines and refrigerators, and coupled experimental and numerical validation of desalination and cooling systems.

**Aerodynamic and renewable energy systems.** Vertical-axis wind turbine optimization and aerodynamic performance enhancement, alongside blended-wing-body UAV design and adaptive flow-control concepts.

## Positions

Previously I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at United Arab Emirates University, a Research Scholar at Stevens Institute of Technology in New Jersey, and a Lecturer and Department Coordinator at the University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore.
